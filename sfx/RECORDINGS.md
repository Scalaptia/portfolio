# Recordings

HARO-PC's body and its screen make real hardware sounds. Everything that runs on the screen
(Stacker, the face editor) makes retro rFXGen sounds instead, from `sounds.txt`.

All recordings are from Freesound under CC0, so no credit is required. They are listed anyway, so a
sound can be traced back and re-cut. Each was cut from the Freesound HQ preview, mixed to mono,
DC-offset removed, faded at both ends, resampled to 32 kHz 16-bit and normalised to the peak given.
